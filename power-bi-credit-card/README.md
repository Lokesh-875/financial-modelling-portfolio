## Page 1 — Transaction Report
- **Revenue by card tier:** Blue cards drove ₹46M (83% of total revenue) vs. Silver (₹6M), Gold (₹2M), Platinum (₹1M)
- **Revenue by expenditure type:** Bills (₹14M) and Entertainment (₹10M) lead, followed by Fuel, Grocery, Food, and Travel
- **Revenue by channel:** Swipe transactions dominate (₹35M) over Chip (₹17M) and Online (₹3M)
- **Quarterly trend:** Revenue stayed broadly flat (₹13.3M–₹14.2M per quarter) while transaction volume peaked in Q3 (166.6K)

## Page 2 — Customer Report
- **By income group:** High-income customers contribute the most revenue (₹22M)
- **By occupation:** Businessmen are the single largest segment (₹17.4M revenue, ₹2.5M interest earned)
- **By age group:** 40–50 age band is the largest revenue contributor (₹25M combined)
- **By geography:** Texas, New York, and California are the top 3 states by revenue
- **By gender:** Male customers (₹30M) contribute more revenue than female customers (₹25M)

## Key Insight
Revenue is heavily concentrated: Blue-tier cards, high-income, businessman-segment customers in the 40–50 age band disproportionately drive revenue. For a bank, this suggests retention and upsell efforts (e.g., moving Blue-tier high-spenders to Gold/Platinum) would have outsized ROI versus broad-based campaigns.

## What this project demonstrates
- Data modelling and relationship-building across transaction and customer tables in Power BI
- DAX measures for revenue, interest, and transaction aggregations
- Dashboard design: slicers (card category, quarter, gender, education) for interactive filtering
- Translating dashboard output into a business recommendation, not just charts
